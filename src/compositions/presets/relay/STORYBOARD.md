# Relay: From feedback to finished

26 seconds, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps. A fictional review-and-handoff product, authored as an illustrative preset. No real customer names, adoption numbers or performance claims. Silent.

The visual direction is warm paper, navy and coral with shallow 2.5D rotation. No browser frame, typography zoom cycle, or data-processing corridor. One launch-film brief survives the entire story.

| Time | Transformation |
| --- | --- |
| 0-4 | Review questions surround an unfinished brief. The type has a stable reading size. |
| 4-10 | Questions converge into the same brief's review rows. Copy, sound and final sign-off are approved in order. |
| 10-15 | An approval stamp lands. The brief holds long enough to read, then folds into a delivery packet. |
| 15-21 | The packet travels along an arc. A trailing lateral camera follows it to the next person's tray. It opens into the same approved brief. |
| 21-26 | The delivered brief becomes the Relay mark. The camera parks on the brand and promise. |

The continuity owner is `relayPaper`, a persistent element outside scene containers. Its own outline, faces and perspective change. Review statuses, SVG checks, material, tray and camera all use one caller-owned timeline with finite animation. No irreversible completion callbacks. The adapter only mounts HTML and calls the timeline.

Reusable lesson: make a decision visible on a persistent artefact, then let that artefact motivate a journey. Copy this cause-and-effect structure for a relevant brief, not the paper envelope or coral palette for every product.
